Release Notes (Stamina Notebook)

Last updated: October 20, 2026

v27 (2026-10-20)

  • The way data is stored has been renewed. Your existing data is carried over automatically the first time you open the app after updating
  • Backup and restore have been reworked
  • Backups created with an older version cannot be restored in this version. Please create a new backup after updating
  • Please open the app once after updating. Until then, widgets may appear empty

  • Home screen widgets now come in four types (next entry, tiny 1x1, list, per game)
  • Widgets show the game's icon image
  • The list fits its height and shows progress bars
  • The next-entry and 1x1 widgets are free (list and per-game are PREMIUM)
  • Fixed the time / "Full" text on the right of widgets being cut off
  • Fixed the dial opening by mistake when you start scrolling on the edit screens
  • The version is now shown in "About this app"
